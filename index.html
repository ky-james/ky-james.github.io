Degen Poker League

Overview

The Degenerate Poker League started as a solution to a recurring problem: every time my friends and I played poker, we’d lose track of who bought in, who cashed out, and what were the results. After one too many chaotic post-game audits, I decided to build a better system.

This project is my pride and joy. It’s the perfect blend of product management and software engineering — I get to design solutions, build them out, and immediately see the impact at every step of the way. There’s honestly no better feeling than watching my friends crowd around the leaderboard, laughing, trash-talking, and genuinely enjoying something I created.

Bison Bump Challenge

What's the Bison Bump Challenge?

The Bison Bump Challenge is a business pitch competition open to AISES college members across the U.S. and Canada, focusing on culturally grounded innovations that are sustainable and community-driven. It seeks bold STEM projects such as robotics, coding, traditional ecological knowledge, and environmental solutions that harness Indigenous wisdom to address today’s most pressing challenges while honoring both tradition and innovation. Teams of up to five are invited to propose ideas with real potential for positive community impact.

My Pitch

Polar Pods is an Indigenous-led business that helps northern communities access fresh food by turning shipping containers into year-round hydroponic greenhouses. Each unit is built to handle the extreme weather and transport challenges of remote regions, growing crops like greens, berries, and herbs locally. The company offers sales and leasing, ongoing support, and community training instead of one-time deals. By working directly with Indigenous partners and helping secure grants, Polar Pods lets communities take charge of their food supply and create new jobs and learning opportunities. Financially, the business is designed for steady growth and aims to deliver reliable food access and lasting value for northern regions.
